# Lux Tenebre Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 14, 2026_

Lux Tenebre is a single-player card game played against computer opponents on your device. This policy explains what information the app handles. The short version: **the app does not collect, send or share any personal data.**

## Information the app collects

None. Lux Tenebre has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no online features. It does not connect to the internet and does not ask for any device permissions (such as location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage).

## Information stored on your device

To remember your preferences between sessions, the app saves a few settings on your device:

- the wizard name you type in Settings (optional; shown only in the game's score screen);
- music and sound volume, and whether sound is muted;
- the language you chose;
- whether you have already seen the "How to play" tutorial and the guided first match.

These settings never leave your device through the app, and the developer cannot see them. You can change them at any time in Settings, or delete them by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

## Device backups

If your Android device has backup turned on, the system may include these settings in your personal backup (for example, Google's backup service), so they can be restored on a new device. That backup is handled by your device and its backup provider under their own privacy policies, not by Lux Tenebre.

## Children's privacy

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Third parties

The app does not share information with third parties, because it does not collect any.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with an updated date.
